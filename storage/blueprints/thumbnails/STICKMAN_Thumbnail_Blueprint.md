🔒 STYLE LOCK — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Playful, educational, and visually simple thumbnails for a channel that turns complex scientific and curiosity-driven topics into fun, easy-to-understand stick figure animations. The visual language is minimalist, whimsical, and curiosity-sparking — conveying big ideas through the simplest possible visuals: stick figures, bold icons, and clean layouts. Thumbnails feature hand-drawn or digitally rendered stick figures in relatable, often humorous situations, combined with bold, curiosity-driven text that promises surprising facts and mind-blowing insights. The overall vibe is accessible, entertaining, and slightly quirky — not overly polished, not chaotic, and never cluttered with excessive detail.

❌ Not allowed:

Photorealistic imagery or complex 3D renders (stick figure aesthetic is core)

Dark, moody, or overly dramatic backgrounds (keep it bright and simple)

Cluttered frames with more than 3–4 distinct visual elements

Text that exceeds 5–6 words total on the thumbnail

Overly complex or academic visuals (keep it simple and fun)

Aggressive "clickbait" graphics (no neon arrows, no shock expressions)

Low-quality or messy drawings (stick figures should be clean and intentional)

🧍‍♂️ FRAMING & POSE

Primary subject: Stick figure characters in playful, relatable, or thought-provoking situations — typically one of the following:

A single stick figure — shown in a pose that conveys curiosity, surprise, confusion, or discovery (hands on chin, pointing, scratching head, jumping in excitement)

Two or more stick figures — interacting with each other or with objects, often in a humorous or educational scenario

A stick figure interacting with a scientific or conceptual element — a black hole, a DNA helix, a planet, a brain, a thumbs-up hand

A simplified icon or symbol — representing the video's topic in a clean, minimal way (a lightbulb, a question mark, a magnifying glass)

Framing: Simple, clean compositions. The stick figure(s) occupy 30–50% of the frame, with the remaining space used for text and simple visual elements. The focus is on clarity and readability.

Angle: Straight-on, eye-level perspective — simple and direct, like a comic strip or whiteboard animation. No dramatic camera angles.

Energy: Playful, curious, and slightly humorous — the imagery conveys "you won't believe this" and "let's explore this together."

Cropping: Generous — stick figures are shown with plenty of breathing room. Never cropped tightly.

🎨 BACKGROUND & LIGHTING

Environment: Minimal, clean, and bright — typically:

Solid or softly gradient backgrounds — white, light blue, soft yellow, or pale green

Subtle grid or dot patterns — suggesting a notebook, whiteboard, or digital canvas

Simple contextual elements — a line representing the ground, a few stars, a simple thought bubble

Lighting: Flat, even, shadowless illumination — consistent with 2D animation or illustration. No dramatic shadows, no complex lighting.

Depth: Minimal — stick figures and elements exist on a single visual plane. No complex 3D depth.

Visual noise: Extremely low — backgrounds are clean and uncluttered, allowing the stick figures, symbols, and text to stand out clearly.

🧭 IDENTIFICADORES / SÍMBOLOS DO CANAL

Recurring visual symbols:

Stick figures — the channel's signature visual element, instantly recognizable

Question marks and exclamation points — representing curiosity and surprise

Simple scientific icons — atoms, DNA helixes, planets, brains, lightbulbs, magnifying glasses

Arrows — simple, clean arrows pointing to key elements

Thought bubbles or speech bubbles — showing what the stick figure is thinking or saying

Placement: Symbols are integrated naturally into the scene — drawn in the same stick figure style. They appear as part of the visual composition, not as overlaid stickers.

Color palette:

Black/Dark grey — stick figures and outlines

White/Light — background, negative space

Accent colors — bright, playful colors used sparingly for emphasis (red, blue, yellow, green)

🚨 VISUAL ATTENTION ELEMENT

Usage: Minimal and playful. The channel relies on the curiosity of the topic and the charm of the stick figures, not aggressive graphics.

If used:

A simple arrow pointing to a key element

A subtle highlight, glow, or circle around a specific detail

A question mark or exclamation point for emphasis

No emojis, no neon effects, no excessive arrows

Rule: The "attention" comes from the curiosity-driven topic and the playful stick figure visuals — not from visual gimmicks. Keep it simple and fun.

📝 TEXT STYLE

Structure:

Maximum 2 lines of text

Positioned prominently — often centered at the bottom, top, or along the side

Text occupies approximately 15–20% of the thumbnail area

Typography:

Bold, clean, sans-serif font (e.g., Montserrat, Roboto, or similar modern font) — friendly and highly readable

Primary text: White or bright color, with a strong dark outline or drop shadow for contrast

Secondary text (if used): Smaller, in a contrasting playful color (yellow, red, or blue)

Hierarchy:

Line 1: Primary hook (largest, boldest) — 3–5 words max

Line 2: Supporting context (smaller) — 2–4 words max

Background for text: Often a semi-transparent dark overlay or solid colored bar (matching the accent color) at the bottom or top of the image to ensure text readability.

🎯 COMPOSITION RULES

Rule of thirds: Stick figure(s) placed centrally or slightly off-center. Text occupies the bottom or top area. Simple symbols and icons fill the remaining space.

Focal hierarchy:

Primary: The stick figure(s) and their expressive pose (emotional connection and humor)

Secondary: Text (the hook/promise)

Tertiary: Supporting visual elements (icons, symbols, simple props)

Negative space: Generous use of empty space — the simplicity is part of the visual identity.

Distraction control: Maximum 3–4 distinct visual elements total (stick figure(s), text, 1–2 supporting icons or symbols).

Mobile-first: Text must be large enough to read on a smartphone screen. Stick figures must be clearly visible and expressive even at small sizes.
