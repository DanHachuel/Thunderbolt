🧍‍♂️ FRAMING & POSE

Primary subject: Conceptual, symbolic imagery — typically one of the following:

A human figure in silhouette — often alone, facing away from camera, or partially obscured, conveying isolation, introspection, or existential contemplation

Abstract or symbolic visuals — representations of philosophical concepts (mazes, mirrors, doors, staircases, eyes, fractals, cosmic imagery)

Everyday objects rendered strange — familiar things presented in unfamiliar, thought-provoking ways

Architectural or geometric forms — stark, minimal structures that suggest order, meaning, or the absence thereof

Natural or cosmic imagery — stars, voids, landscapes that evoke the sublime or the infinite

Framing: Wide, cinematic compositions that emphasize scale, isolation, or the relationship between the individual and the vast. The primary subject occupies 40–60% of the frame, with the remaining space used for text and atmospheric depth.

Angle: Often dramatic — low angles convey awe and insignificance; high angles suggest observation and detachment; straight-on creates a sense of direct confrontation with the idea.

Energy: Contemplative, mysterious, and slightly unsettling — the imagery conveys "there's more here than meets the eye" and "reality is not what it seems."

Cropping: Subjects are framed with breathing room for text placement, typically centered, at the bottom, or on one side.

🎨 BACKGROUND & LIGHTING

Environment: Dark, atmospheric, and minimal — typically:

Dark or black backgrounds with subtle gradients or textures

Moody, low-key lighting with strong contrasts between light and shadow

Minimal, abstract environments — voids, empty spaces, liminal zones

Nighttime or twilight scenes — suggesting mystery and the unknown

Subtle, muted color palettes — deep blues, dark greens, warm ambers, monochromatic schemes

Lighting: Dramatic, chiaroscuro-style lighting — strong contrasts, deep shadows, and selective highlights. Light often appears to be coming from a single source (spotlight, window, cosmic light), creating a sense of revelation or insight.

Depth: Strong sense of depth — layered compositions with foreground (subject), midground (contextual elements), and background (void or cosmic space). Creates a sense of infinite space and existential scale.

Visual noise: Very low — backgrounds are clean and uncluttered, allowing the symbolic imagery and text to stand out with maximum impact.

📝 TEXT STYLE

Structure:

Maximum 2 lines of text

Positioned prominently — often centered at the bottom, lower third, or on the side

Text occupies approximately 15–20% of the thumbnail area

Typography:

Bold, clean, sans-serif font (e.g., Helvetica, Montserrat, or similar modern minimalist font)

Primary text: White or light-colored, with a strong dark outline or drop shadow for contrast against dark backgrounds

Secondary text (if used): Smaller, in a contrasting muted color (amber, light blue, or gray)

Hierarchy:

Line 1: Primary hook (largest, boldest) — 3–5 words max

Line 2: Supporting context (smaller) — 2–4 words max

Background for text: Often a semi-transparent dark overlay or gradient fade at the bottom of the image to ensure text readability. Sometimes text is placed directly on a dark area of the image with no background bar.

🎯 COMPOSITION RULES

Rule of thirds: Primary subject (figure, symbol, object) placed along one of the vertical or horizontal thirds. Text occupies the negative space — often at the bottom or on the side. The background provides atmospheric depth.

Focal hierarchy:

Primary: The conceptual imagery (figure, symbol, object) — the "hook"

Secondary: Text (the promise/hook)

Tertiary: Supporting visual details (atmosphere, light, subtle symbols)

Negative space: Dark, atmospheric space provides natural negative space. Text and subjects are placed to avoid overlapping in ways that reduce readability.

Distraction control: Maximum 3–4 distinct visual elements total (primary subject, atmospheric background, text, optional secondary symbol).

Mobile-first: Text must be large enough to read on a smartphone screen. The primary subject must be clearly identifiable even at small sizes.
