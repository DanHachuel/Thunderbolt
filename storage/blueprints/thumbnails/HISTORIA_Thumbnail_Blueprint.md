Energy: Calm, curious, and informative — the imagery conveys "here's something useful you should know" and "let me explain this simply."

Cropping: Generous — subjects are shown with plenty of breathing room. Never cropped tightly.

🎨 BACKGROUND & LIGHTING

🎯 COMPOSITION RULES

Rule of thirds: Primary subject (icon, illustration, or person) placed centrally or slightly off-center. Text occupies the bottom or top area. Simple symbols and icons fill the remaining space.

Focal hierarchy:

Primary: The subject (icon, illustration, or host) — the visual hook

Secondary: Text — the promise/hook

Tertiary: Supporting visual elements (simple icons, arrows, diagrams)

Negative space: Generous use of empty space — the simplicity is part of the visual identity.

Distraction control: Maximum 3–4 distinct visual elements total (subject, text, 1–2 supporting icons or symbols).

Mobile-first: Text must be large enough to read on a smartphone screen. The primary subject must be clearly identifiable even at small sizes.
