# thumbnailprompt-ShakeMusic-V2.md

🔒 STYLE LOCK — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Dark, moody, and cinematic thumbnails for a Trap & Bass / EDM music label channel. The visual language is **atmospheric, immersive, and visually striking** — conveying raw energy, intense moods, and the feeling of being transported into a powerful sonic experience. Thumbnails feature **high-quality anime/gaming character art** (often sourced from DeviantArt & ArtStation) combined with **intense neon lighting, glowing energy effects, and bold, commanding text**. The overall vibe is **dark, immersive, and gaming-culture adjacent** — not cheerful or bright, not minimalist or academic, and never cluttered with excessive text.

**❌ Not allowed:**
- Bright, cheerful, or pastel color palettes (keep it dark and moody, with vibrant neon accents)
- Cartoonish or playful illustrations (use cinematic, high-quality digital art or semi-realistic anime styles)
- Completely empty landscapes without a strong focal point (character art is preferred)
- Cluttered frames with more than 3–4 distinct visual elements
- Text that exceeds 5–6 words total on the thumbnail
- Soft, elegant, or delicate typography (text must feel powerful and modern)
- Low-quality or generic stock imagery (must look custom and immersive)
- Photographic faces of real people (the channel uses anime/game artwork, not personal branding)

🧍‍♂️ FRAMING & POSE

**Primary subject:** The visual identity is built on **intense anime/gaming character art** — typically one of the following:
- **A close-up of a fierce anime character** — glowing eyes, intense expression, dark aesthetic (e.g., demon/villain or overpowered protagonist)
- **A mysterious hooded or masked figure** — assassin, rogue, or enigmatic character in a dramatic pose
- **A gaming/anime protagonist in action** — wielding energy, surrounded by an aura, or standing in a powerful stance
- **A stylized digital avatar** — headphones, neon accessories, or cyberpunk elements fitting the EDM/Trap culture

**Framing:** Medium to close-up shots. The character occupies **50–70% of the frame**, positioned centrally or slightly off-center to leave room for text. Faces and glowing eyes are prominent.

**Angle:** Dramatic, low-angle shots that emphasize power and immersion. Occasional Dutch angles for tension. Never flat or straight-on.

**Energy:** Intense, atmospheric, and immersive — the imagery conveys "this music will transport you" and "enter a different world."

**Cropping:** The character is sharply focused, with atmospheric background blur. Cropping is tight but leaves room for text overlay.

🎨 BACKGROUND & LIGHTING

**Environment:** Dark, atmospheric, and cinematic — typically:
- **Dark, moody backgrounds** — black, deep blue, dark red, or gradient shadows
- **Neon energy effects** — glowing auras, light rays, or digital particles surrounding the character
- **Urban or dystopian settings** — cityscapes at night, ruins, or surreal landscapes
- **Cosmic or fantasy environments** — space scenes, magical realms, or abstract dimensions

**Lighting:** Dramatic, high-contrast cinematic lighting — featuring:
- **Rim lighting** that outlines the character's silhouette (often in neon green, pink, cyan, or purple)
- **Glowing eyes or energy sources** — intense focal points
- **Volumetric light** — rays of light cutting through darkness
- **High contrast** between deep shadows and bright, saturated highlights

**Depth:** Strong depth of field — the character is sharp and detailed, while the background is blurred or atmospheric, creating a cinematic, immersive feel.

**Visual noise:** Low to moderate — the artwork is the primary visual, with text overlaid. The composition is clean and focused.

🧭 IDENTIFICADORES / SÍMBOLOS DO CANAL

**Recurring visual symbols:**
- **Intense anime/gaming characters** — the primary visual signature
- **Glowing eyes** — a recurring focal point (green, red, blue, or purple)
- **Neon energy auras** — vibrant outlines or floating particles around the character
- **Headphones, masks, or cyberpunk accessories** — fitting the EDM/Trap and gaming culture
- **Dark, atmospheric environments** — blending anime aesthetics with moody digital art

**Placement:** The character is the primary symbol; text is overlaid as the secondary element. There are no stickers, logos, or overlaid symbols — the channel relies on the artwork and text.

**Color symbolism:**
- **Dark/Black** — mystery, intensity, immersion
- **Neon Green/Cyan** — digital energy, gaming culture, electronic music
- **Neon Pink/Purple** — night vibes, emotional intensity, modern EDM

🚨 VISUAL ATTENTION ELEMENT

**Usage:** Minimal. The channel relies on the **character's intense gaze** and the **bold text** to grab attention — not graphic overlays.

**If used:**
- The character's glowing eyes or energy aura serve as the attention element
- Text is the secondary attention element — large, bold, and highly readable
- No arrows, no circles, no emojis, no graphic overlays

**Rule:** The "attention" comes from the **atmospheric character art and the promise of powerful music** — not from visual gimmicks. Keep it cinematic and immersive.

📝 TEXT STYLE

**Structure:**
- Maximum **2 lines** of text
- Positioned prominently — often **centered**, **lower third**, or **bottom center**
- Text occupies approximately **15–25%** of the thumbnail area

**Typography:**
- Bold, modern, sans-serif font (e.g., Montserrat, Bebas Neue, or a similar clean display font) — highly readable and impactful
- **Primary text:** White or bright color, with a strong dark outline or drop shadow for contrast against dark artwork
- **Secondary text (if used):** Smaller, in a contrasting color (neon green, pink, or cyan)

**Hierarchy:**
- Line 1: Primary hook (largest, boldest) — 3–5 words max
- Line 2: Supporting context (smaller) — 2–4 words max

**Background for text:** Often a **semi-transparent dark overlay** or **gradient fade** behind the text to ensure readability against varied artwork. Sometimes text is placed directly on a dark area of the image.

**⚠️ CRITICAL PRODUCTION NOTE:** AI image generators are currently unable to reliably generate perfect typography. **Generate the background artwork WITHOUT text, then add the text as a separate layer in Photoshop, Canva, or CapCut.** This ensures the font, outline, and drop shadow are crisp and professional.

🎯 COMPOSITION RULES

**Rule of thirds:** The character is placed centrally or along the vertical thirds. Text occupies the bottom third or center. The background provides the visual interest and atmosphere.

**Focal hierarchy:**
1. **Primary:** The intense character art (the visual hook — immersive, mood-setting)
2. **Secondary:** Text (the promise/hook)
3. **Tertiary:** Supporting atmospheric details (lighting, effects)

**Negative space:** The artwork provides atmospheric depth. Text is placed to avoid overlapping the character's face or key visual elements.

**Distraction control:** Maximum 3–4 distinct visual elements total (character, text, 1–2 supporting atmospheric effects).

**Mobile-first:** Text must be large enough to read on a smartphone screen. The character's face and glowing eyes must be instantly recognizable even at small sizes.
