- Text that exceeds 5–6 words total on the thumbnail
- Soft, elegant, or delicate typography (text must feel powerful and aggressive)
- Low-quality or generic stock imagery (must look custom and immersive)
- Overly complex scenes that distract from the central figure
- Photographic faces of real people (use anime/game character art, not personal branding)

🧍‍♂️ FRAMING & POSE

**Primary subject:** A dominant, powerful character or figure — typically one of the following:
- **An anime-style warrior or fighter** — in a combat stance, wielding a weapon, or radiating energy (Solo Leveling-style "shadow monarch" aesthetic)
- **A masked or hooded figure** — assassin, villain, or mysterious protagonist
- **A silhouette against a dramatic backdrop** — standing alone, walking away from an explosion, or facing a massive enemy
- **A close-up of a fierce face** — intense glowing eyes, battle scars, or a confident smirk
- **A gaming character** — League of Legends, VALORANT, or similar franchise figures in action poses

**Framing:** Medium to close-up shots. The character occupies **50–70% of the frame**, positioned centrally or slightly off-center to leave room for text. Faces, eyes, and weapons are prominent.

**Angle:** Dramatic, low-angle shots that emphasize power and dominance. Occasional Dutch angles for tension. Never flat or straight-on.

**Energy:** Intense, aggressive, and adrenaline-filled — the imagery conveys "this music will make you feel unstoppable" and "enter your villain era."

**Cropping:** Tight but purposeful — the character fills the frame with enough breathing room for text. Cropping emphasizes intensity and focus. Keep the face and weapon sharp; allow atmospheric background blur.

**Example poses:**
- Drawing a katana with a glowing aura
- Standing on rubble, looking down at the viewer
- Walking away from an explosion, back turned
- Close-up of glowing eyes with energy crackling around the face
- Facing a giant enemy, weapon raised

🎨 BACKGROUND & LIGHTING

**Environment:** Dark, atmospheric, and cinematic — typically:
- **Dark, moody backgrounds** — black, deep red, dark blue, or gradient shadows
- **Explosive or energy effects** — fire, lightning, smoke, magical energy, shadow tendrils behind the character
- **Urban or dystopian settings** — cityscapes at night, ruins, or apocalyptic landscapes
- **Anime-style energy auras** — glowing outlines around the character
- **Abstract dimensions** — floating particles, geometric magic circles, or void space

**Lighting:** High-contrast, dramatic cinematic lighting — featuring:
- **Rim lighting** that outlines the character's silhouette (often in red, purple, blue, or toxic green)
- **Neon or colored light sources** — red, blue, purple, green glows
- **Volumetric light** — rays of light cutting through darkness
- **High contrast** between deep shadows and bright highlights
- **Glowing eyes** — a recurring focal point (red, purple, blue, or gold)

**Depth:** Strong depth of field — the character is in sharp focus while the background is blurred or atmospheric, creating a cinematic, immersive feel.

**Visual noise:** Low to moderate — backgrounds are atmospheric but not cluttered. The character and text dominate.

📝 TEXT STYLE

**Structure:**
- Maximum **2 lines** of text
- Positioned prominently — often **centered at the bottom**, **lower third**, or **along the side**
- Text occupies approximately **15–20%** of the thumbnail area

**Typography:**
- Bold, aggressive, sans-serif or display font (e.g., Impact, Bebas Neue, or a custom "warrior" font) — highly impactful and readable
- **Primary text:** White, bright red, neon color, or gold, with a strong dark outline or drop shadow for contrast against dark backgrounds
- **Secondary text (if used):** Smaller, in a contrasting color (red for urgency, gold for power, neon for energy)

**Hierarchy:**
- Line 1: Primary hook (largest, boldest) — 3–5 words max
- Line 2: Supporting context (smaller) — 2–4 words max

**Background for text:** Often a **semi-transparent dark overlay** or **gradient fade** at the bottom of the image to ensure text readability against dark backgrounds.

**⚠️ CRITICAL PRODUCTION NOTE:** AI image generators are currently unable to reliably generate perfect typography. **Generate the background artwork WITHOUT text, then add the text as a separate layer in Photoshop, Canva, or CapCut.** This ensures the font, outline, and drop shadow are crisp and professional. The final thumbnail should look like a high-end gaming/anime music video thumbnail.

🎯 COMPOSITION RULES

**Rule of thirds:** Character placed centrally or along the vertical thirds. Text occupies the bottom third. Background effects fill the remaining space.

**Focal hierarchy:**
1. **Primary:** The character (the visual hook — intense, powerful, immersive)
2. **Secondary:** Text (the promise/hook)
3. **Tertiary:** Atmospheric background effects (energy, lighting, environment)

**Negative space:** Moderate — the character and text dominate, but atmospheric space around them adds depth and mood. Avoid overlapping text with the character's face or weapon.

**Distraction control:** Maximum 3–4 distinct visual elements total (character, text, 1–2 supporting effects like energy or environment).

**Mobile-first:** Text must be large enough to read on a smartphone screen. The character must be instantly recognizable and intense even at small sizes.
